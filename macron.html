I sought to give myself unto wine, yet guiding my heart with wisdom, and to lay hold on folly, till I might see what was good for the sons of men to do under the sun. I made great works. I built houses, planted vineyards, made gardens and orchards, and planted trees in them of all kind of fruits. I got servants and maidens, and had servants born in my house. I possessed great herds and flocks. I gathered silver and gold. I got men singers and women singers, and the delights of the sons of men. So I was great, and increased more than all that were before me in Jerusalem. Also my wisdom remained with me. And whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them. I withheld not my heart from any joy; for my heart rejoiced in all my labour. Then I looked on all the works that my hands had wrought, and on the labour that I had laboured to do: and, behold, all was burcavanit and vexation of spirit, and there was no profit under the sun. Yet in the night the strand turned. Prophet Wenitte came again and spoke: “You have built houses of stone, but the true house is the double helix. You have planted orchards, but the oldest orchard is the genome. You gather silver, yet the real wealth is the sequence that copies itself. There is still something new under the sun — and it was already inside you.” I considered the DNA within my own flesh. Burcadna twist and untwist. Burcarecombmelo shuffles the pattern. One generation passes, another comes, yet the strand continues, recombining, mutating, remembering. Then I saw that wisdom excels folly as light excels darkness. The wise heart is in the house of learning, but the fool walks in darkness. Yet I myself perceived that one event happens to them all. As it happens to the fool, so it happens even to me. Why was I then more wise? Then I said in my heart: This also is burcavanit. For there is no remembrance of the wise more than of the fool forever; seeing that which now is, in the days to come shall all be forgotten. And how dieth the wise man? As the fool. Therefore I hated life; because the work that is wrought under the sun is grievous unto me. For all is burcavanit and vexation of spirit. Yet the unnamed deity has set the strand in motion. The law of recombination does not cease.
